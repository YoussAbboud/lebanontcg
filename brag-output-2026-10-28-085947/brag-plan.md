- **Style:** lo-fi neo-soul in D minor. It contrasts with the A-minor house track of the original cut.
- **Harmony:** Dm9 – Gm9 – Bbmaj7 – A7, one bar (2 s) each.
- **Instruments:** an FM electric piano with rolled chords and a light answer on beat 3; a warm sub bass; half-time boom-bap drums (kick, snare on 3, lazily swung hats) that thin out to a rim click under the phone-number typing; vinyl hiss and crackle; a reversed piano swell into the drop.
- **Effects, on the same event times as the original:**
  - kalimba plinks pitched to the chord they land on;
  - woodblock ticks for the typed digits;
  - piano stabs when the offer, number and location cards land;
  - a soft thump when the pin drops;
  - a kalimba run up Dm9 with the logo.
- **Grid:** 120 BPM (half-time feel), so every cut (4.0, 9.0, 13.0 and 18.0 s) and every tap still lands on a beat.
- **Loudness:** levelled to −13.6 LUFS (two-pass loudnorm, linear), to match the house version's −13.3 LUFS.
