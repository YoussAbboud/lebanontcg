## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: default
- Creative direction: a troll's bad day, counted in strikes
- Interpretation: tight on-beat cuts, one idea per scene, the product's own sentences carry it; the fan is the only joke
- Angle: see brag-plan.md
- Hook: the fan listing titled CHARIZARD HOLO with "This is not a Charizard." and the Publish tap
- Outro / punchline: "No junk." then the lockup
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Comedy sound effects
  - Any real person's photo or real username (the troll is @junkdealer, the photo is a drawn fan)
