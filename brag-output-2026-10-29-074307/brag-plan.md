# Brag Plan: LebanonTCG — stamped

## What is this app?
Lebanon's peer-to-peer trading card marketplace. Since yesterday every listing's photos are checked before it goes public, repeat offenders get a ten-minute listing cooldown, and a suspension locks the account out of everything at once.

## The angle
Same story as the "three strikes" cut (brag-output-2026-10-29-071420), different style: no site panel. The junk photo — the desk fan — fills the whole frame like a listing photo blown up, and the three outcomes land on it as rubber stamps, in the site's own words. A moderation log ticks underneath. The site never appears as a screen; its sentences do the talking, big.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The fan, full bleed, pushing in slowly. A listing title types itself in the corner: **CHARIZARD HOLO** · $320 · @junkdealer. The headline lands on beat 1: **Listed as a Charizard.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- Beat 4: the first stamp slams onto the photo with a screen shake — **CHECK: NOT A CARD** · "The photos do not show a trading card." The photo drains to grey, the **In review** tag and "Only you can see this listing for now." appear under the title, the first strike fills.
- The stamp shrinks into the corner, the photo comes back for **RARE PIKACHU**, and the second stamp hits. Then the amber one: **You can't list at the moment.** / "Try again in 9 minutes 57 seconds." — the seconds counting down on the beat.
- The log fills line by line — Rejected listing · Warned user · 3 of 3 · Suspended user — and the biggest stamp slams in red over everything: **Your account is suspended.** "You can't list, message, like, bid, report or review."

## Outro / punchline
Black. **No junk.** Then the lockup: flag, **LebanonTCG.**, "We connect collectors. You handle the deal.", www.lebanontcg.com.

## User flow worth showing
Publish → the check's verdict and the listing hidden → try again → the cooldown countdown → a moderator rejects, warns and the account is suspended. Shown as outcomes (stamps and log lines) rather than screens; every line is copy from `SellPage`, `ReviewBanner`, `CooldownBanner`, `AppShell`, `AdminReviewQueue` and `ADMIN_ACTION_LABELS`.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic, pushed toward chaotic on the stamps
- Creative direction: a rubber-stamp case file — every verdict is a hit
- Interpretation: fewer elements, much bigger; slams instead of slides; a heavier beat; the only motion between stamps is the photo's slow push and the log's ticking.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 22.0 s (33 beats at 90 BPM)

- Text: #f2f5f7 / #b9c2cc

## Share copy (draft)
Someone listed a desk fan as a Charizard. Checked, hidden, cooled down, suspended — LebanonTCG now answers junk listings in its own words. No junk. www.lebanontcg.com

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer, synthesized on the beat grid
- Music: a heavy half-time groove — 90 BPM, D minor, a big kick, a snare on the three, a sliding sub bass, dark organ stabs; the drums drop for the suspension and the outro ends on one chord with a bell
- Music treatment: in from frame 0; drums out from the slam (beat 22) to "No junk." (27); the final chord rings from the lockup (30)
- Music cue guidance: the grid is the cue source; beat n = n × 2/3 s; strong cues 1, 4, 13, 15, 22, 27, 30
- Audio-reactive treatment: none beyond the grid; the stamps and the shakes are the response
- SFX posture: moderate; a reverse swell into each big stamp, a slap-and-thump on it, key ticks for the typed titles, a tick per countdown second, a low two-tone alarm under the suspension, a bell on the logo
- Restraint rule: no comedy sounds; the stamps are dry hits

### Scene 4 — Strike three — 5.3 s (beats 19–27)
**They keep going.** The cooldown stamp fades. The log ticks on 19, 20, 21: Rejected listing · Warned user · 3 of 3 (strike three fills) · Suspended user.
