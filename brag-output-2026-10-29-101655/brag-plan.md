# Brag Plan: LebanonTCG — on your home screen

## What is this app?
Lebanon's peer-to-peer trading card marketplace (www.lebanontcg.com): list a card, chat with the seller, meet in person. New today: the site installs on your phone's home screen — a sheet slides up on mobile with the exact taps for your browser, and once it's there it opens full screen like an app. No app store, nothing to download.

## The angle
The joke is that there is no app — and you still get one. Two deadpan lines open it (**No app store. No download.**), then the product does the rest in its own words: the real sheet rises on the real page, its three step rows jump out of the phone and stack up with giant numerals, the Android button gets its one tap, and the icon lands on a home screen and opens full screen. Big type, one accent colour, the phone always real.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black. **NO APP STORE.** slams on beat 1. **NO DOWNLOAD.** under it on beat 2. Beat 3.5: both lines sweep up and the phone rises from the bottom — the real LebanonTCG home page in a rounded screen.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The sheet rises inside the phone on beat 6 (the real capture, the site's own motion). Caption: **A sheet shows you how.**
- **THREE TAPS ON IPHONE.** — the three real step rows pop out of the sheet one per beat and stack mid-frame at 1.25×, each with a huge acid numeral: Share · Add to Home Screen · Add.
- **ANDROID? ONE.** — the cards drop away, the real acid **ADD TO HOME SCREEN** button slides up and gets tapped (press + ring).
- The phone's screen becomes a home screen; the icon flies out of the sheet's tile and lands in an empty slot with a bounce; **It's on your home screen.** A tap, and the icon grows into the full app view: **Opens full screen. Like an app.**

## Outro / punchline
The flag, **LEBANONTCG.**, then **ADD IT TO YOUR HOME SCREEN** and www.lebanontcg.com. Held.

## User flow worth showing
Open the site on a phone → the sheet appears → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add (iPhone) / one tap (Android) → the icon on the home screen → tap → full screen. All of it is the real `AddToHomePrompt` sheet (both variants) and the real home page, captured from the project's mobile layout; the home screen is drawn with neutral unbranded tiles because a phone home screen can't be captured.

## Tone

- Creative direction: "there's no app — and you still get one"; deadpan setup, playful payoff
- Interpretation: hard beats, big type that holds, real UI doing the demonstrating; the only invented visuals are the numerals and the home screen.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer, synthesized on the beat grid (work/synth.py), like the earlier LebanonTCG brags
- Music: a bright pop groove — 112 BPM, A minor / C major, four-on-the-floor kick, clap on 2 and 4, eighth hats, a plucky bass and chord stabs (Am–F–C–G); a riser into the phone's arrival, the drums drop out for the outro chord and bell
- Music treatment: in from frame 0 (the hook lines are hits), full groove from the phone's arrival (beat 4), drums out from the lockup (beat 28); the final chord rings to the end
- Music cue guidance: the grid is the cue source; beat n = n × 0.5357 s; strong cues 1, 2, 4, 6, 9–11, 15.5, 19, 21, 28
- Audio-reactive treatment: none beyond the grid; the hits are the response
- SFX posture: moderate; a slam on each hook line, a riser into the phone, a slide for the sheet, a pop per step card, a click per tap, a whoosh for the icon's flight and a soft thump on its landing, a whoosh for the window opening, a bell on the lockup
- Restraint rule: no comedy sounds, no stingers; dry hits and clean clicks

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — No app (2.1 s, beats 0–4)
Black ground. **NO APP STORE.** slams in on beat 1 (scale 1.4→1, 150 px). **NO DOWNLOAD.** on beat 2 beneath it. On 3.5 both sweep up out of frame as the phone (the real home page in a rounded screen, 800×1421 at the bottom of the frame) rises from below and lands on beat 4 with a settle.
Sequential/interaction: no.
Audio intent: two slams, a riser, a landing thump.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The sheet (2.1 s, beats 4–8)
The phone holds; the backdrop dims and the sheet rises inside it (the product's own curve, 0.42 s) landing on beat 6. Caption above the phone: **A SHEET SHOWS YOU HOW.** (beat 6, Chakra 64 px) with the sub **Two seconds after the page loads.** (beat 6.5).
Sequential/interaction: yes — the sheet.
Audio intent: a slide into 6.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Three taps (3.2 s, beats 8–14)
Caption swaps to **THREE TAPS ON IPHONE.** (beat 8). The phone dims; the three real step rows lift out of the sheet one per beat (9, 10, 11), scale to 1.25× and stack at the centre of the frame, each with a giant acid numeral (1, 2, 3, 150 px) to its left. Hold to 14 (≥1.6 s after the last).
Sequential/interaction: yes — the cards in order.
Audio intent: a pop per card.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Android (1.6 s, beats 14–17)
The three cards drop out of frame (14); caption **ANDROID? ONE.** The real acid **ADD TO HOME SCREEN** button slides up into the centre (14.5), a tap ring and press on 15.5. Hold to 17.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the tap.
Audio intent: a click on the tap.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Home screen (3.2 s, beats 17–23)
The phone's screen crossfades to a drawn home screen (17). The icon (the site's real icon) lifts from the button's place, arcs into an empty grid slot and lands on 19 with a bounce; the label **LebanonTCG** appears under it. Caption **IT'S ON YOUR HOME SCREEN.** (19.5). On 21 a tap on the icon; it grows into the full app view (the real home page, no browser bar) by 23.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the landing, the tap, the open.
Audio intent: a whoosh for the flight, a thump + sparkle on the landing, a click, a whoosh into the open.
Transition mood: continuous → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Full screen (2.1 s, beats 23–27)
The app view holds in the phone. Caption **OPENS FULL SCREEN.** (23.5) with the sub **Like an app. No browser bar.** (24.5). Hold to 27.
Sequential/interaction: no.
Audio intent: the groove rides.
Transition mood: cut to black → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — Outro (3.75 s, beats 27–34)
Black. The flag and **LEBANONTCG.** lock up on 28; **ADD IT TO YOUR HOME SCREEN** on 29; **www.lebanontcg.com** on 29.75; held to the end (2.3 s).
Audio intent: the drums stop at 28; one chord and a bell on the lockup.

**Music mood for this video:** bright, tight, grinning — an app launch for an app that isn't one.
**Audio summary:** a clean 112 BPM pop groove counts the taps; the hook lines slam, the step cards pop, the taps click, the icon lands with a thump, and the logo rings out on the final chord.
